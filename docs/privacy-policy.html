AudEBook Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

AudEBook syncs an audiobook with its eBook on your Android device. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your device. AudEBook has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer never receives your data.

Information the app uses

  • Books and audiobooks you choose. When you import an eBook, a copy is kept in the app's storage on your device so it can be opened later. Audiobook files are read where they already are on your device; the app keeps permission to read the files you picked.
  • Transcripts. To line the audio up with the text, the app turns short pieces of the audiobook into text using a speech recognition model that runs entirely on your device. The audio and the resulting transcripts never leave your device. Transcripts are saved in the app's storage so a book doesn't have to be transcribed again.
  • Reading data and settings. Your bookshelf, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights and settings (such as font size and playback speed) are stored on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal information.
  • It does not send your books, audio, transcripts or reading activity to the developer or anyone else.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not use your microphone, location, contacts or camera.

Internet access

The app uses the internet only when you ask it to: if you add an online book catalog (an OPDS feed) and browse or download from it, the app connects to that catalog's server. That server is run by its own operator under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for online book catalogs you add.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and notifications: to keep playing an audiobook with the screen off and show playback controls.
  • Wake lock and run at startup: added by standard Android libraries the app uses to keep playback and background tasks running. They give no access to personal information.

Backups

If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, stored in your own Google account under Google's terms. The developer cannot access these backups.

Deleting your data

Removing a book from the bookshelf deletes the app's copy of the book and its cover. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all of its data from your device, including transcripts.

Children

AudEBook is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
